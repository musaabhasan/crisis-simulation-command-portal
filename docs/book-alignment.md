# Book Alignment

This portal is aligned with Musaab Hasan's [Artificial Intelligence for Security Culture Transformation](https://www.amazon.com/Artificial-Intelligence-Security-Culture-Transformation/dp/3639876954).

## Primary Chapter

**Chapter 10: Use of Digital Twins and Simulations for Crisis Scenarios**

## Portal Theme

Crisis labs, ransomware exercises, cross-functional decision-making, and resilience rehearsal.

## Operating Workflow

- Design a scenario with business, technical, legal, and communications injects.
- Assign participants, observers, decision owners, and evidence collectors.
- Run the simulation and capture decision timing, assumptions, and gaps.
- Convert lessons learned into owners, deadlines, and retest criteria.

## Practical Translation

The portal turns the book's conceptual guidance into measurable program records: assessment dimensions, weighted scores, initiatives, evidence items, and roadmap steps.
